Questions

Good questions we hear a lot.

If yours isn't here, ask us. We'll give you a straight answer.

Do we need a new website?

Often not. Restructuring your current CMS, connecting it to your other systems or adding AI to what you already have is frequently the smarter move.

Can you work with our existing Sitecore, Umbraco or Optimizely site?

Yes. We upgrade, restructure and support existing Sitecore, SitecoreAI, Umbraco and Optimizely platforms, and extend them with .NET where needed.

Which CMS should we choose?

It depends on budget, team and how much you need out of the box. Umbraco is open source with low running costs; Sitecore and Optimizely suit larger, licensed setups. We will give you an honest recommendation.

How do you add AI safely?

Through a secure .NET layer with access controls. We avoid sending unnecessary or sensitive data to AI services, and people review AI output before it matters.

What does a CMS migration involve?

Content mapped and restructured for the new platform, a front end rebuilt for it, redirects and SEO protected, and clear goals agreed first. We plan it as a project, not a file transfer.

Do you support sites after launch?

Yes. We offer ongoing maintenance, security updates, monitoring and improvements, from the team that built or migrated the site.

Why do you write your own code?

Fewer security risks, nothing breaks on someone else's update, and software you can own for years.
